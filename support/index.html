Support

We're here to help. Email us at support@nutrilmeapp.com and we'll reply within 2 business days.

You can also send us a message from the app: You tab → Send feedback. You'll need a free account to send feedback or add a product.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I learn how to use each feature? Open How to use Nutril Me in the app: it's at the bottom of the Today tab, and on the You tab under Help and legal. It explains scanning, scores, swaps, Snap a meal, calorie tracking, the Coach and your account settings, a few lines each.

How does the Nutril Me score work? Each food gets a score from 0 to 100 based on its nutrition label (sugar, salt, saturated fat, fibre, protein, energy), its additives and how processed it is. Personal care products are scored on their ingredients. Higher is better. On any product, tap Nutril Me scoring method to see how it's calculated. Scores are our independent estimate, not a statement by the manufacturer.

Why doesn't a product show a score yet? If you see Tap to see score, we haven't loaded that product's full details yet. Tap it and we'll work out its score.

Why did a product's score change? A score can change when the product's information is updated, or when we improve how scores are calculated. If nutrition facts or ingredients are missing, the score is an estimate, and products with no nutrition facts can't score above 49.

A product wasn't found. What can I do? Sign in, tap Add this product and take photos of the front and the nutrition label. Once we've checked it, it becomes available to everyone. You can still log it today: on Today, tap Add food → Enter it yourself.

A product's information looks wrong. Open the product, scroll down and tap Report a problem with this product. Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it.

The camera won't scan. Make sure Nutril Me has camera access: iPhone Settings → Nutril Me → Camera. Hold the barcode inside the frame in good light. You can also type the number: on the Scan tab, choose Type.

How many free scans do I get? 7 new products every day, with or without an account. Scanning a product you've scanned before doesn't use one, and product search, browsing aisles and food recall alerts (the bell on the Aisles tab) are free. The app lets you know when you have one free scan left, and the count resets every day at midnight UTC. Product search is free for everyone. Premium includes unlimited scans.

Do I need an account? No. When you first open the app you can continue with Apple, Google or email, or tap Not now and use Nutril Me as a guest: 7 free scans a day, product search, aisles and food recall alerts. A free account keeps your goals, scans and food log on every device, and you need one to send feedback, add a missing product or subscribe to Premium. Already have one? Tap I already have an account.

What does Premium include? Calorie and macro tracking with a personal daily target, a week view, weight log and meal reminders, Apple Health steps and active calories, adding any food by searching everyday foods or typing in the calories, Snap a meal (an AI estimate of a meal's calories and macros from a photo or a description), AI meal plans and grocery lists (up to 10 AI-made plans a week), comparing two products side by side, notes on how each product fits your goal, unlimited scans, saved products, scan history and the offline library. Both plans, monthly and yearly, come with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers.

How is my daily calorie target worked out? Can I change it? If you add your sex, age, height, weight and activity level, the app works out a daily target with a standard formula (Mifflin-St Jeor), adjusted for your goal. Without them it uses the standard 2,000 kcal. You can also set your own target, from 1,200 to 5,000 kcal: on the Today tab tap − or +, or go to the You tab → Nutrition profile → Daily calorie target → Custom. Tap Use automatic to go back. Targets are for adults and are general estimates, not medical advice.

How accurate is Snap a meal? It's an AI estimate, so check it before adding it to your day: portions are hard to judge from a photo. You can pick a portion size (½× to 2×) before adding it. A photo of a nutrition label gives the most accurate result. We don't keep your meal photos.

How do I log food that has no barcode? On Today, tap Add food. Search everyday foods like an apple, rice or chicken (values from USDA FoodData Central), pick a serving and tap Add to today; use + to log more than one. Know the calories already? Tap Enter it yourself and type them in. For a cooked or restaurant meal, Snap a meal estimates it from a photo or a description.

Where is my food log kept? Your Today log, weight log, body details and calorie target are saved to your account, so they follow you to a new phone. A copy stays on your phone, so you can log offline; it syncs when you're back online.

How do the week view and weight log work? On Today, This week shows your calories for each day from Monday to Sunday against your goal line: green is on target, outlined is under and red is over. Tap a day to see its total. Above the chart are your daily average and how many days you were on target, plus your streak of days in a row with something logged. Tap Log weight to add today's weight (one weigh-in a day; saving again replaces it); it also updates your body details, so an automatic calorie target follows your progress. Add again logs a food you've eaten before with one tap.

How do I turn on meal reminders? You tab → Meal reminders. Tap the time next to breakfast, lunch or dinner to change it (choosing a time switches that reminder on), or use the switch, then tap Save. If nothing arrives, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Nutril Me.

How does Apple Health work with Nutril Me? You tab → Apple Health → Connect. Nutril Me reads your steps, active calories and weight to show them next to your goal. It never writes to Apple Health, and this data stays on your iPhone. To change access: Health app → your profile → Apps → Nutril Me.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone? Sign in to the same Nutril Me account, open the Premium page and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel Premium? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Nutril Me → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to the You tab → Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account, scan history, saved products, meal plans, calorie details, Today log and weight log, and clears them from the phone. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it first (see above).

How do I change the language? You tab → Language.

Is Nutril Me medical advice? No. Nutril Me is for general information only. Always read the product label, especially if you have allergies, and talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making big changes to your diet.
