Support

We're here to help. Email us at support@nutrilmeapp.com and we'll reply within 2 business days.

You can also send us a message from the app: You tab → Send feedback. You'll need a free account to send feedback or add a product.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Nutril Me score work? Each food gets a score from 0 to 100 based on its nutrition label (sugar, salt, saturated fat, fibre, protein, energy), its additives and how processed it is. Personal care products are scored on their ingredients. Higher is better. On any product, tap Nutril Me scoring method to see how it's calculated. Scores are our independent estimate, not a statement by the manufacturer.

Why doesn't a product show a score yet? If you see Tap to see score, we haven't loaded that product's full details yet. Tap it and we'll work out its score.

Why did a product's score change? A score can change when the product's information is updated, or when we improve how scores are calculated. If nutrition facts or ingredients are missing, the score is an estimate, and products with no nutrition facts can't score above 49.

A product wasn't found. What can I do? Sign in, tap Add this product and take photos of the front and the nutrition label. Once we've checked it, it becomes available to everyone.

A product's information looks wrong. Open the product, scroll down and tap Report a problem with this product. Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it.

The camera won't scan. Make sure Nutril Me has camera access: iPhone Settings → Nutril Me → Camera. Hold the barcode inside the frame in good light. You can also type the number: on the Scan tab, choose Type.

How many free scans do I get? 7 new products every day, with or without an account. Scanning a product you've scanned before doesn't use one, and browsing aisles is free. Premium includes unlimited scans and product search.

What does Premium include? Comparing two products side by side, notes on how each product fits your goal, FDA food recall alerts, unlimited scans, product search, the Meal-Prep Coach (up to 3 AI meal-prep plans per week), saved products, scan history and the offline library. The yearly plan comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone? Sign in to the same Nutril Me account, open the Premium page and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel Premium? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Nutril Me → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to the You tab → Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account, scan history, saved products and meal plans. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it first (see above).

How do I change the language? You tab → Language.

Is Nutril Me medical advice? No. Nutril Me is for general information only. Always read the product label, especially if you have allergies, and talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making big changes to your diet.
