Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information the Nutril Me app ("Nutril Me", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: support@nutrilmeapp.com.

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Guest use: when you first open the app, we create an anonymous guest account so your scans can be saved. It isn't linked to your name or email.
  • If you create an account: your email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address (and, if you allow it, the name and profile photo) that they share with us. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive a private relay address.

What you do in the app

  • Your scan history, saved products and meal plans.
  • Your nutrition profile: your allergies, diet and goal. It is used only to show goal notes on products and to build Meal-Prep Coach plans that avoid your allergies. It is never used for advertising or shared with anyone else.
  • Products you submit (including the photos you take of them), problem reports and feedback messages.

Purchases

  • Whether you have an active Premium subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details.

Technical information

  • Your IP address and basic request information, used only to keep the service secure (for example to limit abuse and block automated attacks).

Camera

  • The camera is used to read barcodes on your device. Camera images are not stored or sent to us, except photos you choose to submit with Add this product.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health records or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the app: show product scores, keep your history, saved products and meal plans, and sync them across your devices.
  • To create Meal-Prep Coach plans you ask for.
  • To provide Premium and restore your purchases.
  • To send account emails, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • To review submitted products, fix reported problems and answer your messages.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent abuse.

3. Service providers we use

We share information only with companies that help us run Nutril Me, and only what each needs:

Provider What for What they receive
Supabase Hosting, database and sign-in Your account and app data
Apple, Google Sign-in (if you choose them); Apple processes purchases Sign-in and purchase information
RevenueCat Managing subscriptions An anonymous account ID and purchase status
Anthropic (Claude AI) Creating Meal-Prep Coach plans Your coach preferences only (goal, diet, allergies), never your name or email

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Accounts: until you delete your account.
  • Guest accounts: deleted automatically after 30 days without use.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period before being overwritten.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: You tab → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and its data.
  • Access or correct your data: most of it is visible and editable in the app. For anything else, email support@nutrilmeapp.com.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as receiving a copy of your data or objecting to certain uses. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access to each user's data is restricted so only you can see your own history, saved products and plans.

7. Children

Nutril Me is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. International users

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. We rely on their safeguards to protect it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@nutrilmeapp.com
