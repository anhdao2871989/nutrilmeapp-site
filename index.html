Scan. Score. Eat better.

Nutril Me tells you what's really in your food, in a second.

Scan a barcode on food or personal care products and get a clear health score from 0 to 100, with the good and the bad explained in plain language.

What you can do

  • Scan any barcode. Point your camera at a product and get its score instantly.
  • See the good and the bad. Sugar, salt, fibre, protein, additives and how processed it is, at a glance. Personal care products are scored on their ingredients.
  • Find healthier swaps. Better-scoring alternatives in the same category.
  • Browse by aisle. The best-rated cereals, drinks, snacks and more.

Premium: made for you

  • Allergy & diet warnings. Tell us your allergies (milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, wheat, fish, shellfish, sesame and gluten) and your diet (vegan, vegetarian, halal, kosher, gluten-free and more) once. Every product then shows Contains, May contain or Not listed for you.
  • Compare two products. Side by side: score, calories, sugar, salt, protein, fibre, additives and your allergies, with the healthier choice highlighted.
  • Goal notes. See how each product fits your goal, whether that's weight loss, keto, high protein, low sodium or blood-sugar friendly.
  • Product search. Find any product by name before you shop, with your saved products, scan history and offline library.
  • Meal-Prep Coach. Personal meal-prep plans built around your goal, diet and allergies.
  • Food recall alerts. Live FDA recall reports, so you know when a product is pulled.
  • Unlimited scans. Never get stopped in the middle of the aisle.
