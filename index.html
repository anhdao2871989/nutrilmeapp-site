Scan. Score. Eat better.

Nutril Me tells you what's really in your food, in a second.

Scan a barcode on food or personal care products and get a clear health score from 0 to 100, with the good and the bad explained in plain language.

What you can do

  • Scan any barcode. Point your camera at a product and get its score instantly.
  • See the good and the bad. Sugar, salt, fibre, protein, additives and how processed it is, at a glance. Personal care products are scored on their ingredients.
  • Find healthier swaps. Better-scoring alternatives in the same category.
  • Search any product. Find a product by name or brand before you shop, or pick a category to see the best rated.
  • Browse by aisle. The best-rated cereals, drinks, snacks and more.
  • Food recall alerts. Live FDA recall reports, so you know when a product is pulled.

Premium: made for you

  • Snap a meal. Take a photo or describe your meal and get an AI estimate of its calories, protein, carbs and fat, with how it fits your goal.
  • Calorie and macro tracking. A daily target worked out from your body, activity and goal, or one you set yourself and adjust with a tap, and a Today log of everything you add, saved to your account.
  • Your week at a glance. Calories for each day of the week, Monday to Sunday, against your goal line, how many days you hit your target, your streak, and a weight log with your trend.
  • Works with Apple Health. See your steps and active calories next to your goal, and bring in your weight. Plus meal reminders at the times you choose.
  • Compare two products. Side by side: score, calories, sugar, salt, protein, fibre and additives, with the healthier choice highlighted.
  • Goal notes. See how each product fits your goal, whether that's weight loss, keto, high protein, low sodium or blood-sugar friendly.
  • Saved products and history. Your saved products, scan history and an offline library for the shop.
  • AI meal plans and grocery lists. Meal-prep plans built around your goal, diet and allergies, each with a grocery list.
  • Unlimited scans. Never get stopped in the middle of the aisle.
